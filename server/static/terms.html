PACT / DEMONSTRATION

Terms of use

Pact is a hackathon demonstration for exploring how interview evidence can be connected to behaviour contracts and, with client permission, aggregate product-usage signals. It is not a production service.

Demo data and intended use

The Medicare billing office, interview content, usage events, costs and findings shown here are synthetic demonstration values. Do not enter real patient information, personal information or confidential business data. This service is not intended for clinical, billing, compliance or other operational decisions.

Monitoring and integrations

Usage monitoring is off by default and only operates after the client enables it. The Software Factory interaction shown in this demo is simulated and does not connect to a live 8090 service. AI-generated questions and summaries may be incomplete or inaccurate and should be treated as demonstration output.

Availability and warranty

The demonstration is provided as-is, without warranties or a guarantee of availability, accuracy or fitness for a particular purpose. It may be changed or stopped at any time. No production support or service-level commitment is provided.

Responsible participation

Use only synthetic scenarios and obtain consent before recording or transcribing an interview, including a demonstration interview. Do not use the tool for employee performance evaluation or individual monitoring.
