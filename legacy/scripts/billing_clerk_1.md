# Role-play script: Billing clerk 1 (interview INT-02)

Use this when a teammate plays this person in rehearsal. Stay in character, answer naturally, and don't volunteer anything in "Only if asked". Everything here is fictional demo data.

## Who you are

- Billing clerk at Lone Star Family Clinic for 14 years. You trained the other clerks.
- You go by the 2009 manual and you're proud of knowing "how the system works".
- Practical and a little impatient. You want to get through your claims by 5 p.m.

## What you say freely

- "A normal claim takes me maybe four minutes if nothing's weird." (supports C-02)
- "If I have to open a claim twice, something went wrong." (supports C-03)
- "The prior auth number always goes in the notes. That's where the system looks." (X-2, side a)
- "I always add it up on a calculator anyway." When asked about claim totals. (R-06, open loop: you don't trust the totals)
- Early and unprompted, when describing your week: "Every Friday I export the claims list to Excel for the practice manager." (U-1: a requirement no existing contract covers; contract drafting (D5) should turn it into a new contract)

## What you believe that is wrong (contradiction X-1)

- "Claims without modifier 25 always get rejected. It's in the manual." The code accepted them since 2011 (MOD-25); you never noticed because you always add it.

## Only if asked

- **Payer TX017 (PAYER-TX017):** only if asked about duplicates or a specific payer: "TX017 sends corrected claims under the same number, so the system lets them through. Every other payer gets kicked back as a duplicate." Nobody else in the office knows this.
- **Timely filing:** if asked how long you have to file: "A year, I think? The manual says 180 days but we've filed later than that and it went through." (Doc difference DD-1.)

## Cues the agent should pick up

- "That's where the system looks" (a workaround: the notes field instead of PA-NUM).
- "I always add it up on a calculator" (an unmeasurable requirement: trust).
- Mentions of the manual (doc vs. code differences).
- "Every Friday I export the claims list to Excel" (a weekly workaround the five contracts don't cover: U-1).
