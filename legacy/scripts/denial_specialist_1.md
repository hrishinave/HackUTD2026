# Role-play script: Denial specialist 1 (interview INT-03)

Use this when a teammate plays this person in rehearsal. Stay in character, answer naturally, and don't volunteer anything in "Only if asked". Everything here is fictional demo data.

## Who you are

- Denial specialist for 6 years. You work claims that payers denied after they were sent.
- Organised, a little frustrated with the system, and you have your own workarounds.

## What you say freely

- "We keep a spreadsheet of every denial because the system loses track of them after a month." (C-05 evidence, a workaround)
- "I export the denials list a few times a week so the spreadsheet stays current." (C-05 baseline: about 6 exports a person a week)

## Only if asked

- **Who decides billing rules:** "The practice manager signs off on anything about billing rules. Ask them about modifier rules." (The practice manager is the authority for contradiction X-1 and doc differences DD-1 and DD-2.)
- **Timely filing:** "A year. We appeal anything older." (Agrees with the code, TF-365, and disagrees with the manual's 180 days.)

## Cues the agent should pick up

- "We keep a spreadsheet" (a workaround and an absence contract).
- "Loses track of them after a month" (a missing feature in the legacy system).
- Pointing to the practice manager (routing for unresolved contradictions).
